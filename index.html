3. Personal Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum data necessary to provide our educational services:

  • Account & Identity: Full name (optional), registered mobile phone number (used for OTP-based authentication), username, and Telegram User ID (only if you choose to connect via Telegram login).
  • Educational & Performance Data: Mock test answers and submissions, quiz scores, accuracy statistics, time-per-question metrics, bookmarks, flashcard review intervals, and personal study notes.
  • AI-Assisted Content: Text prompts you provide to generate AI study notes via Google Gemini API are processed by Google's AI models. These prompts may be processed on Google's servers. See Section 6 for details.
  • Technical & Diagnostics: Device model, Android OS version, app version number, network connectivity status, and crash/error logs (for bug fixing and app stability).

🔒 What We Do NOT Collect: We do NOT collect Aadhaar numbers, PAN numbers, biometric data, payment card/banking credentials, precise real-time GPS location, call logs, contacts list, photos/camera feed, or IMEI/device identifiers.

4. Purpose & Lawful Basis for Data Processing

Under the DPDP Act 2023, your data is processed on the lawful basis of your explicit, informed, and affirmative consent, and for legitimate service delivery. The specific purposes are:

  • Account Authentication: To verify your identity via OTP and manage secure login sessions.
  • Test & Performance Services: To calculate scores, display personalized analytics, identify weak topics, and manage your learning streak.
  • AI Notes Generation: To generate personalized study summaries using Google's Gemini AI API based on your input prompts.
  • Spaced Repetition Engine: To schedule and optimize flashcard review intervals for better retention.
  • Customer Support: To respond to your queries, grievances, and account-related requests.
  • Security & Fraud Prevention: To detect unauthorized access attempts, bot activity, and protect account integrity.

We do NOT use your data for targeted advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose beyond the services listed above.

In accordance with the DPDP Act 2023, your consent for data processing is:

  • Access Controls: Database access is restricted to authorized personnel with role-based access controls.
  • No Commercial Sale: We strictly do NOT sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal data in any form.
