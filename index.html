3. Information We Collect

We only collect information strictly necessary to provide our educational and test preparation services:

  • Account & Profile Information: Full name, registered mobile phone number, username, and Telegram ID (only if you opt-in for Telegram login/sync).
  • Educational & Test Performance Data: Quiz submissions, mock test answers, test scores, accuracy metrics, time spent per question, bookmarks, and spaced-repetition flashcard review statuses.
  • User-Generated Study Notes: Notes, summaries, and revision sheets saved locally or synchronized with your user account.
  • Technical & Diagnostics Data: Device model, operating system version, app version, network connectivity status, and crash logs to troubleshoot technical bugs and ensure high application stability.

What We DO NOT Collect: We do NOT collect Aadhaar numbers, PAN numbers, biometric information, banking/credit card credentials, or continuous precise GPS location data.

Your information is processed under the lawful basis of your explicit consent and legitimate educational service fulfillment:

  • To authenticate your identity, generate one-time passwords (OTP), and secure your account.
  • To calculate test results, display performance analytics, and recommend weak-topic revisions.
  • To operate the spaced-repetition algorithm for flashcard learning.
  • To provide customer support and respond to user queries.
  • To detect, prevent, and mitigate fraud, bot usage, and unauthorized tampering.

We implement stringent industry-standard technical and organizational security measures:

  • No Sale or Commercial Exploitation: We strictly do NOT sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information to advertisers, third-party marketing agencies, or data brokers.

7. Your Rights, Data Retention & Account Deletion

Under the DPDP Act 2023 and Google Play policies, you have complete sovereignty over your personal data:

  • Right to Access & Portability: You can review your profile, test scores, and analytics directly within the app dashboard.
  • Right to Correction: You can update your profile details anytime in the app settings.
  • Right to Erasure (Account Deletion): You can delete your account and all associated test history at any time:
    1. In-App: Go to Dashboard → Profile → Delete Account and confirm.
    2. Via Support Email: Send a deletion request to Support@quantawerse.online with your registered mobile number or username.

Upon receiving an account deletion request, all personal data, scores, and progress are permanently purged from active databases within 30 days.
